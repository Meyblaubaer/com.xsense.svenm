# App Store Asset Sources

The driver images in this repository are resized derivatives of official
X-Sense product photography. They are presented on a white background in the
Homey-required 500 x 500 and 75 x 75 sizes.

- CO detector: [XC01-M product page](https://www.x-sense.com/products/xc01-m-smart-carbon-monoxide-alarm)
- Smoke detector: [XS01-M product page](https://www.x-sense.com/products/fs31-smoke-alarm-kit)
- Heat detector: [XH02-M product page](https://www.x-sense.com/products/x-sense-smart-heat-alarm-xh02-m-with-10-year-sealed-battery)
- Water leak detector: [SWS0A product page](https://www.x-sense.com/products/x-sense-sws0a41-wireless-wifi-water-leak-detector-kit)
- Temperature and humidity sensor: [STH0B product page](https://www.x-sense.com/products/sth0b-smart-thermometer-hygrometer)
- Door/window and motion sensors: [X-Sense security system product page](https://eu.x-sense.com/products/x-sense-as05-wireless-smart-home-security-system)
- Mailbox alarm: [SMA11 product article](https://www.x-sense.com/blogs/tips/how-to-choose-the-best-mailbox-alarm-for-your-home)

The App Store lifestyle image is the existing X-Sense home-safety image,
cropped to fill the required 500 x 350 and 250 x 175 canvases without borders.
The app icon is an original unfilled vector drawing with a transparent
background, following the Homey icon guidelines.
